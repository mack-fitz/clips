# Orlando video review: where people leave, and why

Video: "Orlando Has 170 Golf Courses. I Played the Worst Rated One" (finished Orlando.mp4, 24:10)

**How I reviewed it:** I didn't watch it the way a person does. I worked from:
- a full timestamped transcript
- loudness measured every half-second
- a frame every 2 seconds (sheets/ on this branch)

I can't hear tone, timing or delivery the way a viewer does, so treat the "is it funny" calls as a careful read, not a final verdict.

**This is a blind prediction.** I picked the drop points from the video alone. I didn't use your retention graph or anything said about where the drop is.

---

## The short version

**The quiet gaps between jokes are not the problem.** You talk almost nonstop. From 0:00 to 9:45 there isn't a single silence longer than 7 seconds. Your joke rate in the golf (3 to 5 a minute) is close to your intro (about 5 a minute).

What does go wrong in the first 6 minutes:

1. **Around 2:00–2:45 the video argues against its own title.** You keep saying the course is nice and you "don't understand the hate".
2. **You don't hit a ball until 3:44.** That's 15% of the video.
3. **At 3:13 the camera jumps from your face to a far-away tripod.** You get small and harder to hear, right when people are already deciding whether to stay.

## My blind prediction: top 3 drop points

### 1. THE big drop (my one best guess): 2:20 to 2:45, center about 2:30

**On screen:** a selfie walk from the clubhouse to the cart, then you in the cart.

**What you say:**
> "This place seems to be nice. So either Orlando has an incredibly high standard for courses or everybody who's leaving reviews in Orlando, um, I mean the cart was a little dirty, but like so far I'm not understanding all the hate. The course is very pretty, beautiful, beautiful, beautiful... I don't know, I'm not understanding the hate so far." (2:12–2:43)

This comes right after:
> "how was this the worst rated?" (1:55)
> "I'm really starting to think I'm at the wrong place." (2:00)

**Why people leave here:**
- They clicked to watch a terrible course. For about 60 seconds you tell them it isn't terrible.
- In the second half of that minute you trail off mid-thought ("or everybody who's leaving reviews in Orlando, um"). You say "I don't know" and "not understanding the hate" twice.
- This is the only stretch in the first 6 minutes with no clear punchline. It reads as you thinking out loud, not as a bit.
- Nothing new is promised to replace the old promise. Viewers who came for "how bad is it" have just been told the answer is "not bad".

### 2. Runner-up: 3:13 to 3:44, center about 3:25 (the first tee)

**On screen:** you're a small figure on a wide tripod shot under a grey sky. You stand, tee up and waggle for about 30 seconds before the first swing at about 3:43.

**What you say:**
> "All of this rain I can already tell is going to be fantastic for all my electronics."
> "I see a ball in the fairway. If I have a bad shot I'm going to... pretend it's mine."
> "No practice by the way."

**Why people leave here:**
- Your face, which is the reason people are watching, gets small all at once.
- The lines are fine, but nothing happens on screen for half a minute.
- By this point the video is 3:44 in and nobody has hit a ball.

The payoff that follows is strong ("Did somebody say what's for breakfast? It's a fucking ball", 3:46). But anyone who left at 3:25 never hears it.

### 3. Third: 0:25 to 0:50 (the Wawa detour)

The hook is good: 1,200 courses, 170 in Orlando, "the worst rated one of them all". Then at 0:25 it switches to:
> "First I'm at Wawa though... my order today was a sausage egg and cheese bagel. You can't really see it."

Then come the ketchup, the Monster and the white jacket. This is the usual intro bleed, made a bit worse because the hook stops for a breakfast order.

### Smaller dips I'd expect later (slow leaks, not cliffs)
- **4:30–4:55:** the Florida "20–30 minute rain rule" and hurricane explanation. It's a long setup for a small payoff.
- **9:45–10:20:** the phone falls over and the mic transmitter cuts out. There's a 15-second gap with no speech (9:48–10:03) and on-screen captions instead of audio.
- **12:00–20:19:** the "par five from hell" runs 8+ minutes on one hole. You say it yourself: "This could have been its own entire YouTube video, just this hole... for your sake I've cut a ton out" (18:50–19:02).
- **About 10:30 onward:** your voice is 10–15 dB quieter than in the intro, with rain over it (see the audio section below).

## Fixes for the next video

All of these keep your rules: every golf shot stays, the swearing stays, and you stay the center of it.

1. **Hit a ball in the first 60–90 seconds.** Open cold on the funniest bad shot, for example the "what's for breakfast, it's a fucking ball" drive. Then jump back to the drive in. Keep the travel and food bits to about 30–45 seconds in total.
2. **Keep the title's promise alive.** If the course turns out nicer than the reviews, say it once, as a joke with a punchline. Then give the video a new goal straight away, out loud. In this video you already had one: "as long as I didn't get double digits on any hole, that was a good round" (17:02). Moved to the first tee, that line gives viewers a reason to stay. Then check in on it through the round.
3. **Cut away from the wide shot during setup.** On the wide tripod shot, keep the swing and the ball flight. Cut the 20–30 seconds of standing before it, or play your lines over a close-up instead. Pre-shot lines should end in a punchline, not trail off.
4. **Shorten the hole you get stuck on.** Show every shot, but trim the walking and the "what shot is this" between them. Put a running shot counter on screen so you can skip the "is this shot eight?" talk. Aim for no hole longer than about 3 minutes.
5. **Fix the sound before you film.** Wear a backup mic or record audio on the phone too, and use a wind cover on the mic. In the edit, raise the quiet stretches so your voice stays about as loud as the intro all the way through.

**Can you fix this video in YouTube Studio?** The Studio editor can only trim and blur. It can't reorder clips, so a cold open isn't possible there. Two small trims are realistic:
- 2:24–2:43, the "um... I don't know, I'm not understanding the hate" ramble. The bathroom and toilet-paper jokes before it stay.
- 0:33–0:48, the bagel, ketchup and Monster details.

Neither trim touches a golf shot or a swear. A thumbnail or title tweak that leans into the rain disaster rather than the course rating could also help, because the video delivers on the rain and not on the bad course.
